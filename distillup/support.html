DistillUp Support

DistillUp is an iPhone app for rating spirits at distilleries, made by Rough Idea Software. It has no accounts and no profiles — you find a distillery, rate what you drank, and that's it.

Get in touch

Questions, problems, feedback, or a distillery that won't show up? Email us and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. DistillUp has no accounts, no sign-in, and no profiles. Community ratings sync through your iCloud account, so you need to be signed in to iCloud on your iPhone for those to appear — but nothing you contribute is ever linked to you.

Why can't I see any distilleries near me?
The nearby list needs location access. You can turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › DistillUp. If you'd rather not, searching for a distillery by name works without it.

A distillery I want isn't listed.
Nearby results come from Apple Maps, and some venues aren't tagged as distilleries. Try searching for it by name — name search isn't restricted to the nearby area.

Can I change or remove a rating?
Yes. Open the distillery, find the bottle under My ratings and open it. Your newest rating always replaces the previous one. To have your anonymous community contributions removed entirely, email us with the distillery and bottles concerned.

Where does my data go?
Your check-in history stays on your device. Only anonymous rating values are shared, so a distillery’s bottles can carry a community average. Anything you write in a note stays on your device and is never uploaded. The full details are in the privacy policy.
