CellarUp

Get Your Drop On.

CellarUp is in development and is not yet available on the App Store.

Rate what you're drinking, at the winery you're drinking it

Find wineries near you, see how everyone else rated what they pour, and add your own rating. That's the whole app.

Everyone's two cents, nobody's name

There are no accounts, no profiles, no usernames, and no feed. Your own history stays on your device. The only thing shared is an anonymous rating value, so a winery's wines can carry a community average — never who rated what.
