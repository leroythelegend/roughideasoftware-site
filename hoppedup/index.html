Get Your Hops On

HoppedUp is a beer rating app with nothing bolted on. No badges, no feed, no followers — just the brewery you're standing in and the beer in front of you.

📍

Brewery first

Open the app and the breweries near you are already there, closest first. Tap one to see its beers, or search by name for anywhere else.

★

Rate in seconds

Beer, style, half a star at a time. Add the ABV and a short note if you want to — or don't. Your ratings are always yours to edit.

🔒

Anonymous by design

No accounts, no profiles, no usernames. Your history stays on your device, and the ratings shared with the community are never tied to you.
